# EasyFind: Auctionator Extension

Your Auctionator shopping lists in EasyFind: search them, add items from any search result, and check them off in Notes.

## Requirements

- EasyFind 4.3.0 or later
- Auctionator

Retail, WoW Forever, Mists Classic, TBC Anniversary and Classic Era.

## Features

- Search your shopping lists and the items on them by name. Type `@shopping` to list them.
- Right-click any item in EasyFind's results (the item catalog, boss loot, your bags) to add it to a list or start a new one.
- With the auction house open, click a shopping list or one of its items in EasyFind's search results to search it in Auctionator.
- Open a list as a checklist in EasyFind's Notes. Items in your bags count toward each quantity, and your edits go back to the list. At the auction house, Ctrl+click an item in it (or pick Search AH on its menu) to search it in Auctionator.
- Hover a list to see its items with their last scanned prices.
- Start a new shopping list from the + menu in Notes, or make any note a shopping list from its list menu: it becomes an Auctionator list too.
- Notes' shopping lists show Auctionator's prices: each line what is left to buy, the list its total, and how old the scan is on hover.

Tip: EasyFind's item catalog, every item in the game, is off by default. It's the easiest way to find what you want to buy. Type `@cat` before an item's name to search the catalog alone, or turn it on for every search under Items > General Catalog in EasyFind's filter menu.
