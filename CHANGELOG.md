# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- Prices: the last scanned auction price at the right of EasyFind's item rows, with a switch in Options > Extensions.
- Add to shopping list: an item row's right-click menu puts the item on one of your lists, or a new one.
- Shopping list search: your lists and their items found by name in EasyFind, each item with what the term asks for.
- Click: at the auction house, a search in Auctionator's shopping tab; elsewhere, the list in the bar or the item looked up. Shift-click links a known item in chat.
- Right-click: search, find, add to another list, remove from the list.
- Scope: `@shopping` lists your shopping lists.
