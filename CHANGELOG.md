# Changelog

## [1.1.1] - 2026-11-04

- EasyFind 5.0.0 made Notes an addon of its own: this extension now lists the EasyFind: Notes Extension as required, so the CurseForge app installs it with this one and your shopping lists in Notes keep working.
- Without the Notes Extension, a chat line says once where to get it, with a link to its page.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-03

- Notes' shopping lists show Auctionator's prices: each line the price of what is left to buy, the list its total, and on hover the price of one and how old its scan is. Needs EasyFind 4.3.0.
- Make any note a shopping list from its list menu and it becomes an Auctionator shopping list too, tagged for the Auction House; make it a checklist again and the list goes, after asking.
- A "Create shopping list" row leads the shopping lists in the search.
- At the auction house, Ctrl+click a shopping list line in Notes to search it in Auctionator's own tab.
- An item the game has not loaded yet keeps its place on the list by the name you typed.
- Now on Mists Classic, TBC Anniversary and Classic Era too.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-29

- Search your Auctionator shopping lists and their items by name from EasyFind's search bar. `@shopping` lists them.
- Right-click any item in EasyFind's results to add it to a shopping list or start a new one.
- With the auction house open, click a shopping list or one of its items in EasyFind's search results to search it in Auctionator.
- Open a list as a checklist in EasyFind's Notes. Items in your bags count toward each quantity, and your edits go back to the list.
- Hover a list to see its items with their last scanned prices.
- Start a new shopping list from the + menu in Notes.
- Retail and WoW Forever.
